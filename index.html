Operator Console

Operator Console is an internal tool used only by YSNK LLC's own team. It reads reporting data from our own Google Ads accounts (spend, clicks, impressions, conversions and conversion value per product) and keyword planning data (search volume and trends), so we can see which of our products and keywords are profitable.

The tool is not offered to the public or to other businesses. It does not create, modify or delete campaigns, and it does not share any data with third parties.
