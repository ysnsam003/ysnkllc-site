Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy describes how YSNK LLC ("we") handles data in Operator Console, our internal operations tool.

Who uses the tool

Operator Console is used only by members of our own team. It is not available to the public.

What data we access

  • Google Ads reporting data from Google Ads accounts that belong to us: spend, clicks, impressions, conversions and conversion value, per campaign and per product.
  • Google Ads keyword planning data: search volume and trends for keywords.
  • Product and order data from our own online stores.

We access Google data only through the Google Ads API, with the permission of our own Google account, and only read reporting data. We do not create, modify or delete campaigns through the API.

How we use and store it

The data is used only to analyse the performance of our own products and advertising. It is stored on a private server that is not reachable from the public internet, and access is limited to our team.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising to individuals.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for our own reporting. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the tool can no longer read Google data. To request deletion of stored data, contact us at the email below.
